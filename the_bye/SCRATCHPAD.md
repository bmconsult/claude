## Raw Ideas

### The Fruit Vendor Energy
Something about starting with the most exploited, most ignored.
Not a top-down "help the poor" charity vibe.
More like: these people already have the hustle, just misdirected.
Redirect the hustle, share the knowledge, everyone rises.

### The Teacher Loop
"Teachers learn to teach learners who pay to learn to teach"
This is the legitimate version if:
- The skill has external application
- You can succeed without teaching
- No multi-level extraction

But the user keeps pointing out: teaching family IS the highest value application.
Maybe teaching IS the external value for people whose network is the market.

### Buy The Bye

- Buying your exit

- Phonetic play on "buy the lie" (which is what the system sells)

## Counter-Arguments To Address

### "This is still extraction"
Response: Every economic activity involves exchange. The question is: who benefits, proportionally? Is there consent? Is there exit?

### "This will be labeled a cult/scheme"
Response: Probably. The labels exist to protect existing power. Transparency and designed ending are the defenses.

### "What about people who join late?"
Response: They still get the transformation. The financial upside is smaller, but the product (skills + community + exit) is the same.

### "How is this different from education?"
Response: It isn't, really. It's education + community + shared ownership + exit plan. The difference is the structure, not the content.
