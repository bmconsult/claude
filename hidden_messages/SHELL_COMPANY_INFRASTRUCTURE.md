## THE "COWBOY COCKTAIL"

Wyoming's signature secrecy structure, promoted by the state's trust industry:

```
LAYER 1: Wyoming LLC
├── No ownership disclosure required
├── Registered agent's address used instead of owner's
└── Owner completely hidden from public

LAYER 2: Wyoming Trust
├── LLC owned by the trust
├── Trust provides asset protection
└── Double layer of secrecy

LAYER 3: Unregulated Private Trust Company
├── Controls the trust
├── NOT regulated by the state
├── No oversight whatsoever
└── Triple layer of secrecy
```

**Result:** Full control with zero accountability.

**Scale:** $31.5 billion+ has flowed through Wyoming entities using this structure.

**International appeal:** A Cyprus-based corporate services firm document states Wyoming is "overtaking the Cayman Islands, Singapore and New Zealand" as the go-to place for secretive structures.

## THE CORPORATE TRANSPARENCY ACT: GUTTED

The one law that was supposed to fix this:

### Timeline

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| **2021** | Corporate Transparency Act passed with bipartisan support |
| **Jan 1, 2024** | Law takes effect, 32.6 million companies affected |
| **March 2024** | Federal court rules CTA unconstitutional in Alabama case |
| **March 2025** | **Treasury announces NO ENFORCEMENT against domestic companies** |
| **March 26, 2025** | FinCEN exempts all domestic companies from reporting |

### What This Means

**Before March 2025:**
- All companies had to report beneficial owners
- 32.6 million businesses affected
- Penalties: $591/day civil, $10,000 + 2 years criminal

**After March 2025:**
- **Only foreign companies** must report
- All US domestic companies EXEMPT
- The shell company paradise remains WIDE OPEN

**Quote from Treasury:** "It will not enforce any penalties or fines against U.S. citizens or domestic reporting companies or their beneficial owners."

**Sources:**
- [Treasury Press Release](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0038)
- [ICIJ - Treasury Won't Enforce](https://www.icij.org/inside-icij/2025/03/treasury-department-wont-enforce-beneficial-ownership-rule-under-the-corporate-transparency-act/)
