## PART 1: FINANCIAL CONTROL

### 1.1 The Money Creation Monopoly

**Who Creates Money:**
- The Federal Reserve creates base money (M0)
- Commercial banks create ~90% of circulating money through loans
- Money = debt. Every dollar in existence is owed to someone, plus interest.
- The system requires perpetual growth to service existing debt. This is structural, not accidental.

**The Federal Reserve:**
- Hybrid public/private structure
- Member banks hold stock (cannot sell, trade, or use as collateral)
- Member banks elect 6 of 9 directors at each regional Fed
- Board of Governors appointed by President, confirmed by Senate
- Profits go to Treasury (after 6% dividend to member banks)
- Key insight: "Independent" means unaccountable to voters
